## R4: Parties and Outstanding took about 18 s per ageing report at 2,000 parties

**What was wrong.** `receivablesAgeing` and `payablesAgeing` made a row for every customer (or supplier), and
`finishAgeing` took each row's total from `ledgerBalanceOn`, which walked every journal entry and line for that one
ledger. The Parties list runs both reports when it opens, and the party page and Outstanding run one. Each row also
found its party with a search of the whole party list (`find` in `ageingRow`, `some` in the "both" filter). On top of
that, `useBooks` rebuilt the books whenever anything in the store changed, so every save started the work again.

**Reproduced.** `reports-performance.test.ts`, "builds the receivables ageing within the budget", failed with
"expected 6175 to be less than 500"; the payables ageing failed with "expected 940 to be less than 500". The review's
17,979 ms was on its own data, with no products and a journal of about 20,000 entries. The books-hook test in
`large-data.test.tsx` failed after a settings save ("expected ... to be ... // Object.is equality": a new books
object).

**Root cause.** One journal scan per row: the work grew with parties times journal lines (2,000 x 78,600 here), plus
two searches of the party list per row. The books hook subscribed to the whole data object, not to the records the
books are built from.

**Fix.**
- `ledgerBalancesOn` gives every ledger's balance on the date from one pass over the journal. It adds each line in
  the same order as the old per-ledger scan, so every total is the same to the last bit, not only to the paisa.
- `receivablesAgeing` and `payablesAgeing` build one map of parties by id, which `ageingRow` and the "both" filter
  use instead of searching the list.
- `useBooks` selects the eight collections it reads (invoices, purchases, expenses, receipts, vouchers, parties,
  ledgers, products) plus the business, so saving a setting (a dismissed notification), a godown or the trash leaves
  the books as they are (shared with Y13).

**Same figures.** Before changing any code, the old code's reports on the large data were recorded as fingerprints:
the receivables ageing (on 31 Mar 2027 and in mid-year), the payables ageing, the month-by-month P&L (full and part
month), the year's P&L, balance sheet and trial balance. They were taken twice from the old code (the second run
passed, so they are stable). With the fix all of them are unchanged: 906 receivable rows totalling Rs 31,34,280 and
200 payable rows totalling Rs 8,09,116, net profit Rs 9,88,027.50.

**Files changed.** `src/core/accounting/reports.ts`, `src/store/hooks.ts`.

**Regression tests.**

## Y13: lists and reports redid all their work on every keystroke and every save

### The month-by-month P&L

**What was wrong.** `monthlyPnl` called `profitAndLoss` once per month, and each call valued the stock twice (opening
and closing). Each valuation built and sorted every stock movement of the business again: 24 full valuations for a
year. Each P&L section also resolved every ledger that moved in the month with `resolveLedger`, which searches the
whole party list for a party ledger, so about 1,900 party ledgers were looked up 6 times a month.

**Reproduced.** "builds the month-by-month P&L within the budget" failed with "expected 1785 to be less than 500"; the
review measured 3,721 ms on its data. The new test of the shared stock function failed with "closingStockValues is
not a function".

**Root cause.** The same stock history was valued from scratch for every month end, and nothing remembered a ledger
once it had been resolved. Twelve valuations take 59-70 ms on this data (scratch run), so 24 take roughly 130 ms. That
suggests, though it was not measured separately, that most of the 1,785 ms went on the repeated party searches.

**Fix.**
- `closingStockValues` (in `valuation.ts`) values the stock on several dates from one sorted list of movements. It
  shares `addMovement` and `valuationLines` with `valueStock`, so each date gets exactly what `closingStockValue`
  gives, opening stock included on every date as before.
- `monthlyPnl` values all opening and closing dates in one call. `profitAndLoss` and `monthlyPnl` now share
  `pnlWithStock`, which takes the two stock values; `profitAndLoss` still gets them from `stockValueOn`, as before.
- `ledgerInfo` remembers each resolved ledger for its set of books (a `WeakMap` keyed by the `Books` object, freed
  with it). That helps every report: P&L sections, trial balance, balance sheet and statements. No caller changes
  the returned objects (checked by search).

**Files changed.** `src/core/inventory/valuation.ts`, `src/core/accounting/reports.ts`.

**Regression tests.** `reports-performance.test.ts`: "builds the month-by-month P&L within the budget", "gives each
month of the month-by-month P&L the figures of that month on its own" (each month's stock change, gross and net
profit equal to `profitAndLoss` for that month; it passed before the fix too), "values the stock on several dates
exactly as on each date alone" (eight dates out of order, including duplicates, dates before any stock and the year
0000; all three valuation methods; no dates), and the fingerprints above. `data-stock-factories.test.ts` (valuation)
and the P&L tests in `accounting.test.ts` stay green.

### The books rebuilt after any change

**What was wrong, root cause, fix.** As in R4: `useBooks` depended on the whole `data` object. It now selects the
collections it reads. A save's `applyBatch` keeps every untouched collection as the same array, so only a change to
those records rebuilds the books.

**Files changed.** `src/store/hooks.ts`. **Regression test.** `large-data.test.tsx`, "are rebuilt when a record they
are built from changes, not when a setting or a godown is saved".

### The invoice list drew every row and sorted again on every keystroke

**What was wrong.** `SalesList` drew a table row (with a status badge) for every matching document, 10,800 here.
Each keystroke in the search box filtered and re-sorted all of them, with `localeCompare` on documents of the same
day, and redrew the table.

**Reproduced.** `large-data.test.tsx`, "opens within the budget, with the newest 200 documents and a button for 200
more", failed with "expected 7037 to be less than 3000". This was one run, with the test's heap raised to 1,600 MB:
under the usual 700 MB limit the test worker crashed during the render, most likely out of memory. The 3,000 ms was
the test's starting budget. "keeps up with typing in the search box" failed with "expected 2739 to be less than 300"
(average per keystroke while typing "Party 0042").

**Root cause.** The sort ran inside the filter step, so it repeated on every change of the search text. Nothing
limited how many rows were drawn.

**Fix.** The documents are sorted once when they change, and the filters then work on the sorted list, which gives
the same order as before. The table draws the newest 200 matching documents, with "Showing 200 of 10800" and a
**Show 200 more** button. Changing the tab, the search text, the period or the status goes back to 200. Search, the
CSV export, Select all and Export PDFs still work on every matching document, as before. The header totals are worked
out once per change of the list, not on every render. The header now says "10800 documents" instead of "10800
shown", since only 200 are drawn.

**Files changed.** `src/features/sales/SalesList.tsx`, `src/help/guide.ts` (one line in the invoice topic).

**Regression tests.** `large-data.test.tsx`, "the invoice list with 10,800 documents (review Y13)": "opens within the
budget, with the newest 200 documents and a button for 200 more" (it also clicks the button and finds 400 rows),
"keeps up with typing in the search box". The F07 test in `payment-screens.test.tsx` (the dollar invoice shows
`$3,900.00` in the list) stays green, and the every-screen e2e sweep opens the list and a document from it in both
storage modes.

### The header "value" added quotations, proformas and challans to sales

**What was wrong.** The All tab's header summed every final document, so quotations, proformas and delivery challans
counted as sales.

**Reproduced.** `large-data.test.tsx`, "the sales figure in the header of the invoice list (review Y13) > adds
invoices, bills of supply and debit notes less credit notes, but no quotations, proformas or challans", failed on the
sample business: "expected '11 shown | value ₹8,77,522.00 | balan...' to contain '| sales ₹6,71,513.00 |'".

**Root cause.** The total took every final document type, with only credit notes treated differently.

**Fix.** On the All tab the header shows "sales": final documents that post to the books (invoices, bills of supply,
debit notes) less credit notes, in the book currency, as before. A tab of one type still shows "value" for its own
documents; the same test checks that the Quotation tab shows the quotations' value.

**Files changed.** `src/features/sales/SalesList.tsx`, `src/help/guide.ts` (one line in the document-types topic).

**Regression test.** The test named above.
