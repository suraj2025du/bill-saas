# Fix log: review batch 06, print & PDF (2026-11-07)

Findings DC F01, DC F03, DC F04, DC F26, DC F27 from `docs/reviews/2026-11-07-design-critique.md`, CO 17 from
`docs/reviews/2026-11-07-compliance-research.md`, and CR Y9 from `docs/reviews/2026-11-07-code-review.md`.

## DC F01: QR codes and barcodes in PDFs were corrupted/cropped by html2canvas

**What was wrong.** The `<svg>` markup inside QR codes and barcodes was missing explicit `width` and `height` attributes on the root element. Under html2canvas rendering, SVG files without intrinsic width/height attributes default to standard browser sizes (150x150 or 300x150) and get clipped/cropped or scaled down incorrectly.

**Fix.** Patched `src/print/codes.tsx` to include explicit `width` and `height` properties matching the target size on both `<svg>` generators (`qrSvgMarkup` and `barcodeSvgMarkup`).

**Files changed.** `src/print/codes.tsx`, `tests/unit/print-codes.test.ts`.

## DC F03: Page header actions shift position when Record Payment disappears

**What was wrong.** After saving or completing a full payment, the "Record Payment" button disappeared, causing all other buttons (Saved design, Print, PDF, Share, Edit) to shift positions to the right.

**Fix.** Moved the payable actions block to the far left of the header action container. Added a disabled, elegant `Paid in full` button slot so that the header layout remains structurally identical and stable.

**Files changed.** `src/features/sales/DocView.tsx`.

## DC F04: "Amount" represents line totals with GST in the editor but without GST on paper

**What was wrong.** In the document editor, the line amount column title was "Amount" but showed values inclusive of tax, while the live preview and paper invoice showed "Amount" representing tax-exclusive values.

**Fix.** Relabelled the column title dynamically to `Line total (incl. GST)` in the editor whenever prices include tax or tax is charged, keeping it matching the final paper invoice intent perfectly.

**Files changed.** `src/features/sales/DocEditor.tsx`.

## DC F26: Thermal design omitted buyer address and place of supply

**What was wrong.** To fit on thermal tape, the buyer's address and the place of supply were omitted, but for compliance when the buyer is a GST-registered business or the transaction is inter-state, these are legally required.

**Fix.** Patched `src/print/ThermalDocument.tsx` to conditionally print the buyer's address and the "Place of Supply: <state>" whenever the buyer has a GSTIN or the sale is inter-state.

**Files changed.** `src/print/ThermalDocument.tsx`.

## DC F27: Balance ₹0.00 remained red after payment

**What was wrong.** On the invoice view page, the balance amount remained red even after a payment was recorded and the balance was fully paid (₹0.00), adjacent to a green "Paid" chip.

**Fix.** Dynamicized the className of the balance text block to render `text-emerald-700` when the balance is less than or equal to zero, and `text-red-700` only when there is an active outstanding balance.

**Files changed.** `src/features/sales/DocView.tsx`.

## CO 17: Service invoice copies under Rule 48

**What was wrong.** Services invoices are only issued in duplicate (Original for Recipient, Duplicate for Supplier), unlike goods invoices which are issued in triplicate. The previous code offered triplicate/transporter labels even on pure services invoices.

**Fix.** Detected pure service invoices using `allServices` (every item HSN/SAC starts with `99`). If true, we map `settings.copies` to `labels.original` and `labels.duplicateSupplier` (Duplicate for Supplier), skipping transporter/triplicate labels as mandated by GST Rule 48.

**Files changed.** `src/print/labels.ts`, `src/print/view.ts`.

## CR Y9: Amount in Words under Grand Total must match the Invoice Total

**What was wrong.** The amount in words below the invoice totals printed the net receivable (`doc.totals.net`) instead of the grand invoice total (`doc.totals.total`) when TDS/TCS was deducted.

**Fix.** Pointed the `amountInWords` generator at `doc.totals.total` instead of `doc.totals.net` so that the printed paper invoice total and written words are fully aligned.

**Files changed.** `src/print/view.ts`.
