## CO 1: Table 4(A)(5) ITC from GSTR-2B

**What was wrong.** `buildGstr3b` took Table 4(A)(5) from every purchase bill and expense dated in the period, without
using GSTR-2B.

**Root cause.** The GSTR-3B engine was not wired to `parse2b` or the GSTR-2B reconciliation logic.

**Fix.**
- `buildGstr3b` accepts an optional `Parsed2b` for the period. When provided, Table 4(A)(5) is built from the GSTR-2B
  B2B invoices and debit notes with ITC available, net of credit notes, following CBIC Circular 170/02/2022-GST.
- Blocked credit (purchase bills marked `INELIGIBLE`, or expenses where `itcEligible` is false) is reversed in 4(B)(1).
- GSTR-2B documents not found in purchase bills or expenses are reversed in 4(B)(2) and listed in `itc2b.notInBooks`.
- Bills and expenses of the period that claim credit but are missing from GSTR-2B are not claimed and are listed in
  `itc2b.notIn2b`.
- Documents marked "ITC not available" in GSTR-2B are placed in 4(D)(2).
- `Gstr3bPage.tsx` adds an **Open GSTR-2B** button, validates the GSTIN and period, displays the **GSTR-2B check for
  Table 4** card, and keeps the books figure only as a clearly warned fallback when no GSTR-2B is open.

**Files changed.** `src/core/gst/returns/gstr3b.ts`, `src/features/gst/Gstr3bPage.tsx`, `src/help/guide.ts`.

**Regression tests.** `tests/unit/core/gstr3b-review.test.ts` ("Table 4(A)(5) from GSTR-2B (compliance row 1)"),
`tests/unit/ui/gstr3b-screens.test.tsx` ("the GSTR-3B page").

## CO 18: Table 4(D) labels

**What was wrong.** Table 4(D)(1) was labelled "Rule 38 (reversal of credit by a banking company)" instead of the
FY 2023-24 form wording, and 4(D) rows were omitted from the screen and Excel/CSV exports.

**Fix.** Renamed 4(D)(1) to `(1) ITC reclaimed which was reversed under Table 4(B)(2) in earlier tax period` and 4(D)(2)
to `(2) Ineligible ITC under section 16(4) & ITC restricted due to PoS rules` (keeping JSON `ty` codes `RUL` and `OTH`),
and rendered 4(D) on `Gstr3bPage.tsx` and in the Excel/CSV tables.

**Files changed.** `src/core/gst/returns/gstr3b.ts`, `src/features/gst/Gstr3bPage.tsx`.

**Regression tests.** `tests/unit/core/gstr3b-review.test.ts` ("Table 4(D) labels (compliance row 18)"),
`tests/unit/ui/gstr3b-screens.test.tsx`.

## CO 26: Negative liability in GSTR-3B

**What was wrong.** When credit notes exceeded sales in a period, negative tax was floored at zero and not carried
forward.

**Fix.** Recomputed the negative liability statement head-by-head across earlier return periods of the same filing
frequency starting from September 2024, set off opening negative liability before ITC, and carried any remaining
negative balance forward.

**Files changed.** `src/core/gst/returns/gstr3b.ts`, `src/features/gst/Gstr3bPage.tsx`.

**Regression tests.** `tests/unit/core/gstr3b-review.test.ts` ("negative liability (compliance row 26)").

## CO 32: Outward reverse-charge sales in 3.1(a)

**What was wrong.** Outward reverse-charge sales were placed in Table 3.1(c) (nil/exempt).

**Fix.** Confirmed from the GSTN system-generated GSTR-3B (which maps GSTR-1 Table 4B to 3.1(a)) and India Compliance:
taxable outward reverse-charge supplies now report their taxable value in Table 3.1(a) with zero tax, and are excluded
from Table 3.2.

**Files changed.** `src/core/gst/returns/gstr3b.ts`, `tests/unit/core/gst-returns.test.ts`.

**Regression tests.** `tests/unit/core/gstr3b-review.test.ts` ("outward reverse-charge sales (compliance row 32)").

## CR Y7: Input tax credit requires a valid supplier GSTIN

**What was wrong.** Expenses allowed claiming ITC without a vendor GSTIN, and forward-charge purchase bills without a
valid supplier GSTIN were included in 4(A)(5).

**Fix.** `computeExpense` clears `itcEligible` unless `isValidGstin(e.vendorGstin)` is true; `Expenses.tsx` disables the
checkbox and explains why until a valid GSTIN is entered; `buildGstr3b` excludes forward-charge bills and expenses
without a valid GSTIN and notes the count; `seed.ts` adds a valid GSTIN to the sample telephone expense.

**Files changed.** `src/store/actions.ts`, `src/features/expenses/Expenses.tsx`, `src/core/gst/returns/gstr3b.ts`,
`src/data/seed.ts`, `tests/unit/data/seed-trash.test.ts`.

**Regression tests.** `tests/unit/core/gstr3b-review.test.ts` ("input tax credit needs a valid vendor GSTIN (review
Y7)"), `tests/unit/ui/gstr3b-screens.test.tsx` ("the expense dialog (review Y7)").
