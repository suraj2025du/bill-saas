# Fix log: review batch 05, TDS, TCS and income-tax helper (2026-11-07)

Findings CO 2, CO 5, CO 6, CO 20, CO 21, CO 22 and CO 23 from `docs/reviews/2026-11-07-compliance-research.md`, Y10 from
`docs/reviews/2026-11-07-code-review.md`, and F29 from `docs/reviews/2026-11-07-design-critique.md`.

## CO 2 & CO 22: TCS 206C(1) rates from 1 Apr 2026 and No-PAN rate

**What was wrong.** Section 206C(1) used undated rates (1%, 2%, 5%) and had no No-PAN option. Under Finance Act 2026
(Income-tax Act 2025 s.394(1) Sl. 1-5), liquor, tendu leaves, timber/forest produce, scrap and minerals all have a
uniform 2% rate from 1 April 2026.

**Fix.** Added date-filtered rates on `SectionDef` (`getSection(code, date)` and `sectionsFor(kind, date)`): uniform 2%
from `2026-04-01`, legacy 1% / 2% / 5% up to `2026-03-31`, plus `NOPAN(5)` on 206C(1) and `NOPAN(20)` on 195.

**Files changed.** `src/core/tds.ts`, `src/features/sales/DocEditor.tsx`.

## CO 5, CO 6 & CO 21: 2025 Act section codes in CSV, non-resident Form 27Q / Form 144, and Form 168 label

**What was wrong.**
- Quarterly statement CSVs always wrote 1961 Act section codes (`194C`, `206C(1)`), whereas from 1 April 2026 the Income
  Tax Department requires the 2025 Act section / table serial numbers (`393(1) Sl. 6(i)`, `394(1) Sl. 1-5`, etc.).
- Non-resident TDS under section 195 was grouped into the resident `DEDUCTED` register (Form 26Q / Form 140) instead of
  Form 27Q / Form 144.
- Customer-deducted TDS was labelled only `Form 26AS / AIS`, omitting `Form 168` from Tax Year 2026-27.

**Fix.**
- Added `newCode` on each `SectionDef` and `sectionCodeFor(code, date)`, used in `tdsCsv`.
- Added register `DEDUCTED_NR` for section 195 deductions, routed to `Form 27Q` (up to 31 Mar 2026) and
  `Form 144 (earlier 27Q)` (from 1 Apr 2026), and added the tab on `TdsPage.tsx`.
- Updated `statementFor('SUFFERED', date)` and `ItrPage.tsx` to reference `Form 168` for Tax Year 2026-27.

**Files changed.** `src/core/tds.ts`, `src/core/tdsReports.ts`, `src/features/gst/TdsPage.tsx`, `src/features/itr/ItrPage.tsx`.

## CO 20 & CO 23: ITR filing due date default (31 August for business) and 2025 Act section labels

**What was wrong.** Default filing date was always 31 July even when business/profession income was selected (non-audit
business/profession cases are due 31 August from AY 2026-27), and Tax Year 2026-27 showed only 1961 Act section numbers.

**Fix.** Added `defaultFilingDate(fy, businessMode)` returning `${end}-08-31` when `businessMode !== 'NONE'` and
`${end}-07-31` otherwise; updated `ItrPage.tsx` and `incomeTax.ts` to show both 1961 and 2025 Act references (`s.58`,
`Schedule XV (s.123)`, `s.156`, `s.425`) when `fy === '2026-27'`.

**Files changed.** `src/core/itr/incomeTax.ts`, `src/features/itr/ItrPage.tsx`.

## CR Y10: Business loss must not be set off against salary (s.71(2A))

**What was wrong.** `computeRegime` added `businessIncome` directly to `salary - standardDeduction + hp + otherSources`,
allowing a business loss to reduce taxable salary.

**Fix.** Applied business losses only against non-salary heads (`Math.max(0, hp + otherSources + businessIncome)`),
keeping `netSalary` untouched, and updated the warning message to cite s.71(2A).

**Files changed.** `src/core/itr/incomeTax.ts`.

## DC F29: Income-tax helper recommended a regime on an empty form

**What was wrong.** Before any income was entered, the summary banner declared "Better for you: New regime / You save
₹0.00" and badged the card "Lower tax".

**Fix.** When no income is entered, the banner shows "Enter your income to compare"; when both regimes produce the same
tax, it shows "Same tax in both regimes" and hides the "Lower tax" badge.

**Files changed.** `src/features/itr/ItrPage.tsx`.
